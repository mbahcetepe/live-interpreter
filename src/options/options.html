Recommended setups and tips

Four setups that work well, each with what it needs. Apply one to choose its services; your keys and voices stay as they are, and anything can be changed below afterwards.

Tips

  • Wear headphones. Without them the translation you hear leaks into your microphone and goes back to the call; if you have none, turn on Speaker mode.
  • Choose 0.5 s of silence and speak in short sentences: a sentence is translated once you pause.
  • On a video or radio tab, choose the language it speaks if you know it; it saves the first sentences from being heard in the wrong language.
  • With an Anthropic key set, Claude Haiku translates the sentences Ollama, OpenAI, OpenRouter or Gemini could not.
  • The panel shows where the time goes (listening, translation, voice): change the service of the slowest step.
  • The panel's cost estimate counts ElevenLabs and Claude only; the other services bill in their own consoles.
