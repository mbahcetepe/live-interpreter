Live Interpreter speech server

Speech recognition (Whisper) and voices (Piper) for the Live Interpreter extension, on this Mac instead of a paid service. The audio stays on your network.

  • On Apple silicon, Whisper runs on the GPU (MLX).
  • The install downloads Python, the packages and the voices (about 2 GB) and takes a few minutes. The Whisper model (about 1.6 GB) is downloaded on the first start, in the background.
  • It starts by itself at every sign-in. Other computers on your network can use it with its key.
