The speech server is installed

The address and key opened in TextEdit. In the extension: Settings › Speech recognition and voices › Service: My own speech server, enter them and press Test. The first test can wait for the Whisper model to finish downloading.

They are kept in ~/Library/Application Support/LiveInterpreterSpeech/connection.txt; the log is in logs/server.log there.

Remove it: "~/Library/Application Support/LiveInterpreterSpeech/install-mac.sh" --uninstall
